<h3>Steven &amp; Mia Kang Leadership for Social Change Award | Jeneen Barakat</h3>

<h4><img alt="" src="/sites/studentleadership.ucmerced.edu/files/documents/e38ff2b3-95e0-41dd-a598-fe906a645b6c.jpg.jpg" style="width: 350px; height: 401px; margin-left: 10px; margin-right: 10px; float: left;" />The past year Jeneen has lead the first student led protest at UCM to advocate against the genocide in Gaza.
