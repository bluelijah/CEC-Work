		<p>The Yosemite Leadership Program (YLP) strives to be the premier student environmental leadership program in the nation. YLP fosters synergy among government, business, and academia to develop thoughtful, ethical, and innovative leaders.</p>
		<p>In partnership with the National Park Service, the Yosemite Education Center, and the Margo F. Souza Student Leadership Center at UC Merced, YLP educates and inspires environmental advocacy and social change through skilled, intentional, and community-centered leadership.</p>

		<h3 style="color:#2f3a8f; margin-bottom:15px;"><strong>Year One: Leadership Seminar</strong></h3>
		<p style="font-weight:600;">Applications for the 2024&ndash;2026 cohort will open in <strong>August</strong>.</p>
		<p>The Fall 2025 Cohort Leadership Seminar will meet on <strong>Wednesdays from 3:00 PM &ndash; 4:30 PM</strong>, from <strong>September 17, 2025 through November 19, 2025</strong>.</p>
		<p>The first year consists of a series of interconnected elements that help participants explore their leadership identities, the Social Change Model of Leadership Development, community engagement, National Parks, environmental stewardship, and personal growth.</p>
		<p>Sessions are held weekly on campus, and the program includes <strong>two overnight retreats to Yosemite National Park</strong> at the beginning and end of the academic year.</p>

		<p>The Yosemite Leadership Program (YLP) strives to be the premier student environmental leadership program in the nation by fostering collaboration among government, business, and academia to develop thoughtful, ethical, and innovative leaders.</p>
		<p>In partnership with the National Park Service, the Wilderness Education Center, and the Margo F. 	<div style="background:#eef2ff; padding:45px; border-radius:18px; margin-bottom:45px; box-shadow:0 6px 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.06);">
		<h3 style="color:#2f3a8f; margin-bottom:15px;"><strong>Year Two: Social Change Practicum</strong></h3>
		<p style="font-weight:600;">Applications for the <strong>2025&ndash;2027 cohort</strong> will open in <strong>August</strong>.</p>
		<p>The Fall 2025 Social Change Practicum will meet on <strong>Wednesdays from 4:30 PM &ndash; 6:00 PM</strong>, from <strong>September 11, 2025 through November 19, 2025</strong>.</p>
		<p>The second year of YLP focuses on a community-based, applied capstone project supported by practical leadership development curriculum. Participants engage in mentorship with local high school students, complete community volunteer hours, and attend skill-building workshops.</p>
		<p>Projects are developed in partnership with community-serving organizations and UC Merced student groups, providing students with real-world experience in designing, executing, and evaluating impactful initiatives. The program also includes <strong>two overnight retreats to Yosemite National Park</strong> during the academic year.</p>
