			<p>The Yosemite Leadership Program Summer Internship (YLPSI) is a full-time, immersive experience designed to prepare students for leadership roles in environmental stewardship, public lands management, and civic engagement.</p>

			<p>Through hands-on work with the National Park Service and partner organizations, interns gain professional skills, deepen their leadership capacity, and contribute meaningfully to Yosemite National Park and surrounding public lands.</p>

			<h2>Eligibility Requirements</h2>

			<p>To be accepted into the Yosemite Leadership Program Summer Internship, applicants must:</p>

			<ul>
				<li>Be a current UC Merced student <em>or</em> a former participant in a Yosemite youth program (e.g., Youth Conservation Corps, NatureBridge, WildLink, Parks in Focus, Youth Ancestral Stewards).</li>
				<li>Be available for the full 12-week internship from <strong>May 18 &ndash; August 7, 2026</strong>.</li>
				<li>Be at least 18 years old by May 18, 2026.</li>
				<li>Be a U.S. citizen or U.S. permanent legal resident.</li>
				<li>Be willing to complete a background check and drug testing.</li>
				<li>Possess, or be willing to obtain, a valid driver&rsquo;s license with a clean driving record.</li>
			</ul>

			<p><em>Note: To qualify for non-competitive Direct Hiring Authority (DHA) with the federal government, interns must be enrolled as a student during the internship and in Fall 2026. Students graduating in Spring 2026 may apply but are not eligible for DHA.</em></p>

			<h2>Internship Opportunities</h2>

			<p>Applicants rank their internship preferences during the application process.</p>

			<ul>
				<li>Bear Diet Researcher &ndash; Wildlife Management</li>
				<li>Medical Clinic Centennial Historian &ndash; Yosemite Medical Clinic / NPS Archives</li>
				<li>Communications Intern &ndash; Yosemite Conservancy</li>
				<li>Cultural Resource Intern &ndash; Division of Resources Management &amp; Science</li>
				<li>Indian Cultural Demonstrator &ndash; Interpretation &amp; Education</li>
				<li>Merced River Ecological Restoration Intern &ndash; Vegetation &amp; Restoration</li>
				<li>Plant Ecology Associate &ndash; Vegetation &amp; Ecological Restoration</li>
				<li>Tribal Relations Intern &ndash; Cultural Resource Branch</li>
			</ul>

			<h2>Application Components</h2>

			<ul>
				<li>Completed applicant information and ranked internship preferences</li>
				<li>Resume</li>
				<li>Written responses (1&ndash;2 pages total)</li>
				<li>Proof of academic good standing (transcript or grade report)</li>
				<li>Optional but encouraged letter of recommendation</li>
			</ul>

			<p><strong>Written response prompts include:</strong></p>

			<ul>
				<li>Why are you interested in the Yosemite Leadership Program Summer Internship?</li>
				<li>What leadership traits do you bring, and when have you demonstrated them?</li>
				<li>Describe a time you worked on a team and helped achieve shared goals.</li>
				<li>Describe a time you stepped outside your comfort zone to pursue a meaningful goal.</li>
			</ul>

			<h2>Submission &amp; Questions</h2>
