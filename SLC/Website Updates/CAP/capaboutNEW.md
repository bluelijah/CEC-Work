      <h2>Program Overview</h2>
      <p>The Margo F. Souza Leadership Center's Chancellor's Ambassadors Program cultivates a select group of 6&ndash;8 student leaders who represent the excellence, diversity, and potential of UC Merced. Chancellor's Ambassadors serve as official student representatives at major campus functions, alumni gatherings, VIP visits, donor events, and external engagements, reinforcing the university's mission, vision, and community impact.</p>
      <p>The program is rooted in the values of leadership, service, and institutional pride, creating a dynamic bridge between students and the broader UC Merced community. Selected students work <strong>6&ndash;8 hours per week</strong>.</p>

      <h2>Program Benefits</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Professional Development:</strong> Specialized training in public speaking, event protocol, donor engagement, institutional storytelling, and leadership.</li>
        <li><strong>Career Preparation:</strong> Exposure to university leadership, alumni, donors, and public officials to grow professional networks and job readiness.</li>
        <li><strong>Travel &amp; Representation:</strong> Opportunities to represent UC Merced at conferences, alumni functions, and community initiatives.</li>
        <li><strong>Prestige &amp; Recognition:</strong> Formal recognition that enhances resumes and professional portfolios.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Eligibility Criteria</h2>
      <p>To be considered for appointment as a Chancellor's Ambassador, students must meet all of the following criteria:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Currently enrolled UC Merced undergraduate or graduate student</li>
        <li>At least one academic year remaining at time of appointment</li>
        <li>Minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA</li>
        <li>Good student conduct standing</li>
        <li>Valid driver&rsquo;s license</li>
        <li>Successful completion of a DMV pull and background check</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>How to Apply</h2>
      <p>Students are nominated to the Chancellor's Ambassadors Program. Nominations open each fall semester. When the nomination form is live, use the button below to submit your nomination.</p>
