		<h2 id="cap-title" style="text-align:center; color:#003a5d; margin-bottom:20px;">Program Overview</h2>

		<p style="font-style:italic; font-size:16px; line-height:1.7; color:#333;">The Margo F. Souza Leadership Center introduces the Chancellor&rsquo;s Ambassadors Program. This program will cultivate a select group of 6&ndash;8 student leaders who represent the excellence, diversity, and potential of UC Merced. Chancellor&rsquo;s Ambassadors students will serve as official student representatives at major campus functions, alumni gatherings, VIP visits, donor events, and external engagements, reinforcing the university&rsquo;s mission, vision, and community impact. The program is rooted in the values of leadership, service, and institutional pride, creating a dynamic bridge between students and the broader university community.</p>
