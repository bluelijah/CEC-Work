<h2 style="text-align: center;"><strong>2025 Leadership Awards </strong><em>(Self-nominations permitted)</em></h2>

<p>The following awards are open to self-nominations and nominations made by others, providing an opportunity for individuals to showcase their own leadership, contributions, and impact within the UC Merced community. These awards celebrate students who take pride in their achievements and wish to be recognized for their dedication, innovation, and service, Self-nominations allow students to highlight their unique journey and the ways they&#39;ve made a difference on campus and beyond. Winners will be invited to attend the 2025 Leadership Awards Ceremony on Friday, April 25th, 2025, where they will be celebrated for their achievements and honored for their contributions.&nbsp;</p>

<hr />
<h3 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Distinguished Leadership Award</strong></h3>

<p>The Distinguished Leadership Award recognizes&nbsp;<strong>undergraduate student(s)</strong>&nbsp;who demonstrated exceptional leadership skills in the&nbsp;<strong>past academic year.</strong>&nbsp;The individual(s) have made a significant strides within the&nbsp;<strong>UC Merced communtiy</strong>&nbsp;through their exemplary leadership and changemaking in action. More than one recipient may be awarded.&nbsp;<strong>Self-nominations are permitted for this award.&nbsp;</strong></p>

<h3 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Legacy Award</strong></h3>

<p>The Legacy Award recognizes a <strong>student graduating during the 2024-2025 academic year (undergraduate or graduate)</strong> who epitmizes &quot;best of leadership&quot; qualities within the UC Merced community through actions consistent with the&nbsp;<a href="http://www.ucmerced.edu/principles-of-community">University&#39;s Principles of Community</a>. These principles include upholding a culture of dialogue and civility through civic engagement and environmental stewardship, while celebrating identities, values, and beliefs while affirming each person&#39;s dignity. The recipient of this award will be an individual whose actions and ideals displayed throughout their time as a student at UC Merced will leave a lasting, postive impression on the institution while rasing the standard of excellence for future generations.&nbsp;<strong>Self-nominations are permitted for this award.</strong></p>

<h3 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Emerging Leader Award</strong></h3>

<p>The Emerging Leader Award recognizes <strong>first-year and first-year transfer students </strong>who have demonstrated exceptional leadership, initiative, and impact during their inaugural year at UC Merced. These individuals embody the spirit of leadership, dedication, and service; contributing meaningfully to their campus community through their actions, involvements, and/or influence. This award celebrates the promise and potential of emerging leaders, inspiring others to make thei rmark on the UC Merced community. <strong>Self-nominations are permitted for this award.</strong></p>

<h3 style="text-align: center;"><strong>College Corps Fellow Award</strong></h3>

<p>The College Corps Fellow Award honors a <strong>Fellow</strong> who has demonstrated exceptional leadership and dedication to Merced and neighboring communityies throughout the <strong>2024-2025 academic year.</strong> This award recognizes a Fellow who has consistently participated in all program activities and shown a strong commitment to supporting other program fellows, their host site and the population they serve. The recipient of this award exemplifies the values of the College Corps program by actively engaging in service to address societal challenges while embodying the spirit of civis responsibility and leadership. The individual&#39;s efforts have benefitted the program and have had a lasting impact on the Merced community. <strong>Self-nominations are permitted for this award.&nbsp;</strong></p>
