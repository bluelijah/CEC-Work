	<!-- WHAT WILL I DO -->

	<h2 style="text-align:center; margin-bottom:15px;">What Will I Do in This Program?</h2>

	<div style="max-width:900px; margin:auto; font-size:16px; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:30px;">
		<p>In this program, you will learn the importance of your role as a student leader and how your work contributes to student retention and sense of belonging.</p>

		<p>Participants will engage in self-reflection exercises and dialogue with other student leaders to share resources, experiences, and build networks of support.</p>

		<p>Participants will learn what it means to work in a team and learn different approaches to team dynamics.</p>

		<p>Participants will understand how to plan and organize events using a limited budget.</p>

		<p>Participants will learn how to manage and operate budgets.</p>

		<p>Participants will learn strategies for dealing with conflict resolution, delegation, and member accountability.</p>

		<p>Participants will learn how to communicate with students, campus partners, and university stakeholders to build coalitions and networks of support.</p>

		<p>Participants will understand the importance of mentorship and servant leadership to empower and inspire new students to become leaders.</p>
	</div>
