      <ul>
        <li><strong>Workshop 1:</strong> Introduction to Leadership: How Student Leadership Impacts Student Retention &amp; Sense of Belonging</li>
        <li><strong>Workshop 2:</strong> Team Dynamics: Working With A Team</li>
        <li><strong>Workshop 3:</strong> Event Planning 101</li>
        <li><strong>Workshop 4:</strong> Finances &amp; Budgeting</li>
        <li><strong>Workshop 5:</strong> Overcoming Challenges: Conflict Resolution, Delegation, &amp; Accountability</li>
        <li><strong>Workshop 6:</strong> Leadership in Action: Mentorship &amp; Coalition Building</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>What Will I Do in This Program?</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Learn the importance of your role as a student leader and how your work contributes to student retention and sense of belonging.</li>
        <li>Engage in self-reflection exercises and dialogue with other student leaders to share resources, experiences, and build networks of support.</li>
        <li>Learn what it means to work in a team and explore different approaches to team dynamics.</li>
        <li>Understand how to plan and organize events using a limited budget.</li>
        <li>Learn how to manage and operate budgets.</li>
        <li>Learn strategies for dealing with conflict resolution, delegation, and member accountability.</li>
        <li>Learn how to communicate with students, campus partners, and university stakeholders to build coalitions and networks of support.</li>
        <li>Understand the importance of mentorship and servant leadership to empower and inspire new students to become leaders.</li>
      </ul>
